About Us

Unique Solutions For Developing and Maintaining (HSE) Culture in all our Operations.

THOM SANG is an independent local Equatoguinean company authorized and registered with the Ministry of Mines and Hydrocarbons (MMH) as a services provider and Onshore/Offshore supply chain services: We are committed to providing professional service to clients and partners, today and in the long term. Our goal is to fill the service gap for the Oil and gas industry in Equatorial Guinea and in many other fields of interest to our clients.

Our business model is focused on providing multiple fully integrated services in the country for our customers support. We have teams available 24/7 to respond to any customer requests or queries.
